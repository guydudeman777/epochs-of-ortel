# Third-party art

Characters' heads and bodies are built by `tools/art/build_mh_head.py` and `tools/art/build_mh_body.py` from data files distributed with [MPFB](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2). MPFB's code is GPL and is not used or shipped. Only its data files are read.

## CC0, no conditions

- MakeHuman base mesh and targets, MakeHuman Community
- The `game_engine` rig and its weights, and the `female1605`, `male1591` and `proxy741` body meshes, which `tools/art/build_mh_body.py` builds the bodies and clothes from
- `makehuman_system_assets`: skins, eyes, brows, lashes, teeth, tongue and hair
- `skins01` and `skins02`: skins by MargaretToigo and OnlyTheGhosts
- `bodyparts05`: beards by rehmanpolanski, grinsegold, wdg and culturalibre

Check `eyelashes01` and `faceunits01` before shipping either. The game doesn't currently use them, and `faceunits01` states no licence.
